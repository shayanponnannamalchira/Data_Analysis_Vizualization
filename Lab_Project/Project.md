Project Synopsis: Customer Shopping Behavior and Trends Analysis

1. Project Overview
The objective of this project is to perform Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) and data visualization on retail consumer data to uncover purchasing patterns, demographic trends, and the impact of promotional discounts. Understanding consumer habits allows businesses to optimize inventory, refine marketing campaigns, and improve overall customer segmentation.

2. Recommended Dataset

* Dataset Name: Shopping Trends and Customer Behaviour Dataset

* Description: Contains around 3,900 customer transaction records with 18 key attributes, including customer demographics (age, gender), purchase details (item bought, category, amount spent, location, season), and behavioral factors (payment methods, shipping type, frequency of purchases, and discount usage).

3. Key Objectives & Scope

* Data Cleaning & Preprocessing: Handle missing values, check for duplicate records, and standardize column types.

* Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA): Analyze spending distributions across different age groups, genders, and product categories.

* Visualization Dashboard: Build intuitive charts (correlation heatmaps, distribution plots, and category breakdowns) to communicate core business insights effectively.

4. Packages Used

* Data Manipulation & Cleaning: `pandas`, `numpy`

* Data Visualization: `matplotlib`, `seaborn`

* Development Environment: Jupyter Notebook / Google Colab

5. Expected Outcomes

* Identification of top-performing product categories and peak shopping seasons.

* Clear insights into how discounts and promotional codes influence consumer spending amounts.

* A comprehensive visual report and summary deck to guide strategic retail decision-making.
